Rulebook

Objective

Collect the most Prestige Points (Stars) by the time the game ends. The game ends when either all Map Cards are flipped (including both Property Auctions) or all Luxury Cards are sold AND both Property Auctions are held.

Gameplay

Players take turns flipping Map Cards to earn Gold. Gold is then used to buy Luxury Cards (Points) or bid on Properties (Big Points).

Card Types

  • Block (3 Au): Safe. Automatic success.
  • Waterfront (4 Au): Safe. Completing a column starts an Auction.
  • Atlantic (6 Au): Medium Risk. Must roll 3-6 to succeed.
  • Lagoon (8 Au): High Risk. Must roll 4-6 to succeed.
