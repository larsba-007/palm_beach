# Boca Raton: Rules

*Working rules, 16 Nov 2026, updated 17 Nov with Lars's answers and the character card print files. Based on the printed **Rulebook BOCA RATON v4**, with three updates:*
1. *Bonus cards are no longer gold foil. **Bonus and landmark cards show their extra gold as a yellow circle (+1, +2 or +5) in the upper left corner.***
2. *The **luxury cards are played open**: the top card of each pile is face up, as in the web game.*
3. *The **character powers** follow the character card print files (4 Nov 2026), which replace the appendix in rulebook v4.*

*Items marked **[CHECK]** are things Lars should confirm before this becomes the new rulebook text.*

2–5 players · Ages 14+ · 45–60 minutes

---

## Objective

Welcome to Boca Raton, one of the most exclusive destinations in the world, home to historic figures and modern-day celebrities. You take on the role of one of these characters, competing for status. The player with the most **Prestige Points** at the end of the game wins.

You earn Prestige Points in two ways:
- **Luxury Cards** (1–3 Prestige Points each), bought during the game.
- **Property Cards** (2–4 Prestige Points each), won at the two Property Auctions.

The currency is gold, written **Au**. The acrylic diamonds are not used in Boca Raton.

## Components used

- 2 Main Boards (Boca Raton side up)
- 56 of the 64 Map Cards
- 2 of the 3 Auction Map Cards
- 3 Property Cards: Mar-a-Lago (IV), Gulf Stream Golf Course (III), Spanish River Park (II)
- 17 Luxury Cards
- 10 Character Cards (Boca Raton side)
- Wooden counters (10 × 10 mm): 15 red (powers), 17 yellow (re-rolls), 8 blue (extra turns)
- Gold bars: 15 × 10 Au, 15 × 5 Au, 30 × 1 Au
- 1 die

*No Player Aids (removed from the game, Lars 17 Nov 2026): the die roll and gold are printed on the map cards and the bonus circles show the extra gold, so the cheat sheet isn't needed. Setup is in this rulebook.*

## Setup

1. **Boards and Map Cards.** Place the two Main Boards in the middle of the table, Boca Raton side up. Sort the Map Cards by the type on their backs, shuffle each type, and lay them face down on the matching spaces, as in the setup diagram: rows 1, 4, 5 and 8 are full rows of 9 cards (Lagoon, Waterfront, 5 Block, Waterfront, Atlantic); rows 2, 3, 6 and 7 have 5 cards (Lagoon, Waterfront, Block, Waterfront, Atlantic), leaving room in the middle for the 2 Auction Map Cards. That is 56 cards. Return the 8 leftover Block cards to the box unseen.
2. **Auction Map Cards.** Shuffle the 3 Auction Map Cards face down. Take 2 at random and return the third to the box unseen. Place one between rows 2 and 3, and the other between rows 6 and 7.
3. **Luxury Cards.** Shuffle the 17 Luxury Cards and make three face-down piles on the board: the **15 Au pile with 5 cards**, and the **10 Au and 5 Au piles with 6 cards each**. **Turn the top card of each pile face up** and lay it on top of its pile. The rest of the pile stays face down, so nobody sees the next card even if the pile slides. *(As in the web game; Lars, 17 Nov.)*
4. **Property Cards.** Put the 3 Property Cards to the side, for example on one of the face-down Auction Map Cards.
5. **Bank.** Put all gold bars on the board as the Bank. **Everyone starts with 0 Au.**
6. **Characters.** Shuffle the 10 Character Cards and deal 2 to each player. Each player keeps one and returns the other to the box. Read the **Boca Raton** abilities, not Palm Beach. Reveal at the same time and take any starting counters and gold shown on your card. (The initiative numbers R1–R8 are only used in Palm Beach.)

*First game tip:* deal one character per player instead of two.

*How to flip a card:* turn it like the page of a book, so the picture faces the right way for everyone.

## Gameplay

Each player rolls the die; the highest roll starts. Play goes clockwise.

On your turn, choose **one** action:
1. **Flip a Map Card**, or
2. **Acquire a Luxury Card.**

### 1. Flip a Map Card

Choose any face-down Map Card on the board. The card back shows its type:

| Type | Die roll | Reward |
|---|---|---|
| **Lagoon**: high risk, big reward | Roll 4–6 | 8 Au |
| **Atlantic**: medium risk | Roll 3–6 | 6 Au |
| **Waterfront**: auction trigger | None | 4 Au |
| **Block**: safe and steady | None | 3 Au |

**On a failed roll** you get nothing, and the card stays face down.

**Bonus and landmark cards (new).** Some cards have a **yellow circle in the upper left corner** of the front, showing extra gold. When you flip one, take the extra gold as well:

| Bonus | Cards | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **+5 Au** | 9 Block Bonus Cards | Block cards |
| **+2 Au** | 8 landmark cards | Lagoon, Waterfront (Lagoon side), Block and Atlantic |
| **+1 Au** | 4 landmark cards | Waterfront (Atlantic side) |

A landmark card's story is told in *The World of Boca Raton & Palm Beach*.

These counts are for all 64 Map Cards. Boca Raton uses 56: the 8 Block cards left out are random and unseen, so some bonus cards may not be in play. *(Lars, 17 Nov)*

*Example:* you flip a Block card with a +5 circle and collect 3 + 5 = 8 Au.

### 2. Acquire a Luxury Card (open cards)

The top card of each pile is face up, so you always see the three luxury cards on offer and their Prestige Points. Choose one:

| Pile | Cost | Die roll |
|---|---|---|
| **15 Au** | Pay 15 Au | None: take the card |
| **10 Au** | Pay 10 Au | Roll 3–6 |
| **5 Au** | Pay 5 Au | Roll 5 or 6 |

If you fail the roll, you lose the gold you paid and don't get the card.

When a card is taken, **turn the next card of that pile face up** (only the top card is ever face up). When a pile is empty, it is out of the game.

*The heart of Boca Raton:* which of the three visible luxury cards is worth it, and do you pay full price for a sure thing, or pay less and trust the die?

## Events

Two events can happen. They interrupt play immediately.

### Row and column bonus
When the last card of a row or column is flipped, the player who flipped it gains **5 Au**. A row only counts if it is a full row of 9 cards (rows 1, 4, 5 and 8). *(Lars, 17 Nov; v4 said "8 Map Cards".)*

### Property Auction
There are two Property Auctions in the game.
- **Trigger:** an auction starts as soon as a player flips the last **Waterfront** card in a Waterfront **column**. The left Waterfront column (Lagoon side) starts the **top** auction (the Auction Map Card between rows 2 and 3); the right Waterfront column (Atlantic side) starts the **bottom** auction (between rows 6 and 7). *(Lars, 17 Nov. The v4 text says "rows" where it means these columns.)*
- **Reveal:** flip the matching Auction Map Card. It shows which Property Card is auctioned. Some characters collect gold before certain auctions; they take it now.
- **Bidding:** the player who flipped the last Waterfront card bids first. Bidding goes clockwise. Raise by at least 1 Au, or pass. Once you pass, you are out. Bidding ends when one bidder remains.
- **Winning:** the winner pays the Bank and takes the Property Card. The other Property Cards wait for the second auction.

## End of the game

The game ends when either:
1. all Map Cards are face up, or
2. all Luxury Cards and Property Cards have been acquired.

## Final scoring

Add up the Prestige Points on your Luxury Cards and Property Cards. The most points wins.

**Tiebreaker:** the tied player with Mar-a-Lago wins. Otherwise, the tied player with the most gold left wins. If still tied, share the victory.

## Counters

Character cards show three kinds of counters:
- **Red, Powers:** spend one to use your character's power.
- **Yellow, Re-rolls:** spend one to re-roll the die after a roll.
- **Blue, Extra Turns:** spend one to take another turn.

**When to use them (Lars, 17 Nov):** Powers and Extra Turns are used **at the start of your turn**: you can't decide to use them after you've taken your action. Re-rolls can be used whenever you roll, as many as you have. A power with 0 red counters on the card works all game without counters.

**Clarified (Lars, 17 Nov):**

- **Ms. Post and Carina** place their own red Power counters (3 each for the whole game).
- **Carina:** a counter stays on the Map Card until someone rolls a 5 or 6 and flips it. Carina herself flips it without rolling ("All except Carina must roll 5 or 6").
- **Leja and Mr. Flagler:** the old v4 "peek" powers are replaced by the card texts above.

## FAQ

**What is public and what is secret?** Your gold is secret. Counters, Luxury Cards and Property Cards are kept in view, so everyone can count each other's Prestige Points.

**Is Eric's ability his whole turn?** Yes. Flipping the three Waterfront cards is his action.

**Can Silviano take two actions?** Yes. By spending a counter he takes his normal action and also acquires a Luxury Card.

**Do I get the landmark bonus on a failed roll?** No. You only get bonus gold when the card is flipped.
