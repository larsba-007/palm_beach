## +2 Au landmarks

### Ocean Ridge Natural Area
*Lagoon · +2 Au · Ocean Ridge, Palm Beach County*

This 27-acre natural area in Ocean Ridge preserves two native Florida ecosystems: maritime hammock and mangrove swamp. Accessible by boat, bicycle, or on foot, it provides habitat for wading birds, juvenile fish, and other coastal wildlife.

### Henry Morrison Flagler Museum
*Waterfront (Lagoon side) · +2 Au · Palm Beach*

Completed in 1902 as Whitehall, Henry Flagler's Gilded Age estate in Palm Beach, this National Historic Landmark is now the Flagler Museum. It preserves and interprets Whitehall, Henry Flagler's legacy, and America's Gilded Age.

### Little Red Schoolhouse
*Block · +2 Au · Palm Beach*

Founded in 1886 as a community project, the Little Red Schoolhouse was the first schoolhouse in southeast Florida. Today it stands in Palm Beach as an important link to the area's earliest settlers and educational history.

### Pan's Garden
*Block · +2 Au · Palm Beach*

Established in 1994 by the Preservation Foundation of Palm Beach, Pan's Garden is Florida's first all-native botanical garden. It takes its name from the bronze statue of Pan of Rohallion at the entrance, designed by Frederick MacMonnies in 1890.

### The Church of Bethesda-By-The-Sea
*Block · +2 Au · Palm Beach*

Founded in 1889, Bethesda-by-the-Sea is one of Palm Beach's oldest congregations. Visitors can explore its rich history, stained glass, intricate woodwork, and the peaceful Cluett Memorial Gardens & Koi Pond.

### The Society of the Four Arts
*Block · +2 Au · Palm Beach*

Founded in 1936, The Society of the Four Arts is a nonprofit cultural organization dedicated to artistic and educational programming. Its Palm Beach campus includes gardens, libraries, exhibitions, concerts, lectures, and other cultural programs.

### Gumbo Limbo Nature Center
*Block · +2 Au · Boca Raton*

This 20-acre coastal preserve on Boca Raton's barrier island is surrounded by a rare remnant of coastal tropical hammock. It is known for its boardwalk through the coastal forest, butterfly garden, and 40-foot observation tower.

### Deerfield Beach International Fishing Pier
*Atlantic · +2 Au · Deerfield Beach*

The 976-foot Deerfield Beach International Fishing Pier is one of the city's signature attractions, drawing visitors and anglers throughout the year. The pier offers fishing access, rentals, bait, tackle, and sweeping views of the Atlantic Ocean.

## +1 Au landmarks

### Ocean Front Beach
*Waterfront (Atlantic side) · +1 Au · The Gold Coast*

Located along the Atlantic coast, this beachfront area represents the open shoreline that defines the Gold Coast. It offers direct access to the ocean and reflects the coastal setting that has shaped communities throughout Palm Beach County.

### Ocean Ridge Hammock Park
*Waterfront (Atlantic side) · +1 Au · Ocean Ridge*

Ocean Ridge Hammock Park is a small, tranquil stretch of Atlantic shoreline in Ocean Ridge. Known for its quiet setting, palms, and ocean views, it offers a peaceful place to relax and enjoy the natural beauty of the coast.

### Gulf Stream Golf Club
*Waterfront (Atlantic side) · +1 Au · Gulf Stream*

Founded in 1924, Gulf Stream Golf Club is a private, invitation-only club with a course designed by legendary golf architect Donald Ross. Its Mediterranean Revival clubhouse was designed by Addison Mizner.

### Atlantic Dunes Park
*Waterfront (Atlantic side) · +1 Au · Delray Beach*

Atlantic Dunes Park in Delray Beach is a beachfront park with a boardwalk, nature trail, pavilion, picnic tables, public restrooms, and lifeguard supervision. It offers a scenic and accessible way to enjoy the Atlantic shoreline.
