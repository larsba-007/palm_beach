## Bonus circle colours

White numbers need a dark enough background. Contrast with white text (WCAG; 3.0 is the minimum for large bold text, 4.5 for normal text):

| Colour | RGB | Hex | CMYK (FOGRA39) | Contrast with white |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current yellow | 252/203/6 | #FCCB06 | 4/20/95/2 | **1.5**: too low |
| **Sunset orange** (main) | 214/90/40 | #D65A28 | 0/74/92/13 | **3.9** |
| **Roof red, deep** (test A) | 200/30/20 | #C81E14 | 0/97/100/15 | **5.8** |
| **Plum** (test B), palette 216 | 112/80/103 | #705067 | 50/67/31/37 | **6.9** |

### Why these three
The cards are mostly green (lawns, trees, golf), blue (water, pools) and sand (beaches). A bonus circle should stand apart from all three.
1. **Sunset orange:** warm and clearly "gold/bonus", the opposite of the greens and blues. Close to palette 231 (roof in sun) but darker, so the white number reads.
2. **Roof red, deep:** a darker version of 181 (the bonus red, Pantone 485C), so it belongs to the game's own colours. Strongest contrast of the warm colours. Risk: it can blend with red roofs on Block cards.
3. **Plum (216):** already in the palette, rare on the map (only a few roofs), very readable. Calmer and less "bonus-like" than orange.

### Avoid
- **Green** (229, 233, 235, 237): blends with lawns and trees.
- **Blues and navy** (204, 205, 227, 238): blend with Lagoon and Atlantic water.
- **Yellow:** too little contrast with white.
